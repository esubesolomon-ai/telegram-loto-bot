# Ethio Lottery — Draft Player Terms (Review Required)

**Demo status:** This version does not accept or process real payments and does not pay cash prizes. Ticket prices are displayed only for product testing.

1. **Eligibility:** Participation in any future real-money version is restricted to people meeting the applicable legal age, location, and eligibility requirements. Required identity checks must be completed where applicable.
2. **Draw types and displayed prices:** Daily 100 ETB; Weekly 50 ETB; Monthly 50 ETB. These are proposed display values, not an offer to sell real-money tickets.
3. **Ticket validity:** Each ticket has a unique receipt code and a six-digit number from `000000` through `999999`. Leading zeros are significant. A ticket is tied to one draw only.
4. **Payment confirmation:** In a future approved real-money version, tickets may be confirmed only after the approved payment provider independently verifies the transaction. Screenshots or user claims alone are not proof of payment.
5. **Number uniqueness:** Each six-digit number can be assigned once per draw. If the number pool is exhausted, the system must stop issuing tickets for that draw.
6. **Random result:** The winning number is generated using the documented cryptographically secure random procedure after sales close. The operator must not reroll because no ticket matches.
7. **No-match rule:** Must be explicitly published before sales open. The demo records an unmatched result without rolling over or paying a prize.
8. **Draw interruption or error:** Pause the affected draw, preserve logs and records, investigate, and publish the resolution. No silent changes or undisclosed rerolls.
9. **Refunds and cancellation:** A future real-money version must publish the refund policy for duplicate charges, failed payments, canceled draws, and technical errors before launch.
10. **Prizes and payouts:** A future version must publish the prize amount/funding, winner verification, claim deadline, tax treatment, and payout process. Payouts must be independently authorized and traceable.
11. **Responsible participation:** Do not promise guaranteed profits. Implement legally required age controls, limits, self-exclusion, and help information before launch.
12. **Privacy and complaints:** Collect only necessary data, protect it, and provide a published support channel and documented dispute process.
13. **Legal compliance:** These terms are a draft and do not replace required licenses, approvals, tax obligations, or payment-provider authorization. Obtain Ethiopian legal review before any real-money use.
